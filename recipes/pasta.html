Pasta

Description

Pasta is a delicious Italian dish made from boiled pasta and mixed with tasty sauces, vegetables, cheese, and spices. It is easy to prepare and perfect for lunch, dinner, or a quick snack.

Ingredients

  • Pasta
  • Water
  • Oil or butter
  • Salt
  • Garlic
  • Onion
  • Tomato sauce
  • Capsicum
  • Chilli flakes
  • Black pepper
  • Oregano
  • Grated cheese (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water until soft, then drain it.
  2. Heat oil or butter in a pan and sauté garlic, onion, and capsicum.
  3. Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, chilli flakes, and oregano.
  4. Mix in the boiled pasta, add cheese if desired, and serve hot.
