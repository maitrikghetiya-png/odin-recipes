Sandwich

Description

A sandwich is a delicious food made with fresh bread filled with vegetables, cheese, sauces, and other tasty ingredients. It is perfect for breakfast, lunch, or a quick snack. 🥪

Ingredients

  • Bread
  • Butter
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Cucumber
  • Cheese
  • Green chutney
  • Salt and black pepper
  • Mayonnaise (optional)

Steps

  1. Take two slices of bread.
  2. Spread tomato sauce over it.
  3. Slice the tomato, onion, and cucumber.
  4. Place the vegetables on one slice of bread.
  5. Add cheese, salt, and black pepper.
  6. Spread mayonnaise if desired.
  7. Cover with the second slice of bread.
  8. Cut the sandwich into two pieces and serve fresh.
